AutoEq data sources

autoeq_index.tsv links to the public jaakkopasanen/AutoEq results repository:
https://github.com/jaakkopasanen/AutoEq

harman_in_ear_2019v2.csv preserves the supplied header identifying
AutoEq targets/Harman in-ear 2019.csv.
harman_over_ear_2018.csv preserves the supplied header identifying
AutoEq targets/Harman over-ear 2018.csv.

These three files were extracted from the user-supplied Resonada 2.2.0.2
APK and copied without alteration. The upstream AutoEq project's MIT
license is included in LICENSE-AutoEq.txt. Individual measurements and
brand names retain their respective attribution and rights.

Harman curves are frequency-response references, not headphone correction
gains. The app applies the selected headphone's downloaded AutoEq preset,
including its preamp, without claiming that the reference-target selector
retargets an already computed preset.
