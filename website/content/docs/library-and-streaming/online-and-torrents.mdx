title: Online media and torrents
description: Open direct streams, supported online sources, magnet links, and torrent files responsibly.
---

The unified media hub can save and play direct HTTP/HTTPS streams, HLS playlists, supported online video URLs, magnet links, and `.torrent` files.

:::warning[Use media you are allowed to access]
Torrent and online-source support is transport functionality. You are responsible for the legality of the content and for complying with the source's terms.
:::

## Direct URLs

Use **Open URL** for a receiver-accessible media URL such as an MP4 file or HLS playlist. Authentication cookies, expiring signatures, DRM, and browser-only playback can prevent a URL from working in a native player.

## Supported online sources

The yt-dlp bridge resolves supported websites. Download and mpv video workflows
expose preferences for codec, resolution, frame rate, HDR, container and audio
bitrate. Site changes can temporarily break extraction until the tooling is
updated.

On the experimental ExoPlayer branch, eligible audio uses separate metered and
unmetered bitrate preferences in **Settings > Audio**. Webpage sources can be
resolved to a fresh audio URL on load, preload or retry; declared direct streams
and internal network proxies do not need webpage extraction. This still depends
on source support, valid authentication and an available format. Failed audio
does not automatically switch to mpv.

See [Audio streaming quality](/docs/playback/audio-and-music#streaming-quality-and-cache)
for recovery and cache behavior. These audio limits do not replace the download
format selector or video-quality settings.

See [Downloads and online quality](/docs/library-and-streaming/downloads-and-online-quality)
for destination selection, active/completed queues, and quality-filter guidance.

If a source fails:

1. Confirm the URL opens in a browser.
2. Remove strict quality filters.
3. Try a common container and codec combination.
4. Check the newest mpvRx release notes for extractor updates.
5. Do not post private, authenticated, or expiring URLs publicly.

## Torrent playback

The native torrent engine prioritizes pieces sequentially for streaming. Multi-file torrents provide episode filtering and sorting, watched indicators, and filename-based season/episode parsing.

Playback startup depends on metadata availability, healthy peers, piece distribution, selected-file size, and network policy. A torrent with many listed peers can still stall when the required opening pieces are unavailable.

## Saved media metadata

Saved online items can use TMDB enrichment for posters, backdrops, release information, and summaries. Automated matching is best-effort; verify ambiguous titles and episode numbers before relying on generated metadata.
