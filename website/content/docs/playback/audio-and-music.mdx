mpvRx includes an audio-focused library and now-playing experience in addition to video playback.

## Audio playback

Use the track selector to choose embedded audio streams. Audio delay can correct a fixed synchronization offset. Pitch correction and a configurable volume-boost ceiling are available for playback-speed and quiet-source workflows.

:::warning
Volume above 100% applies digital gain and may clip. Lower the boost when distortion appears.
:::

## Audio focus

**Audio settings → Ignore audio focus** lets audio and video keep playing alongside
other apps without pausing or ducking in response to Android audio focus changes.
It is off by default and can also be found through settings search.

Changing this setting takes effect during foreground and background playback.
Enabling it releases any existing audio focus request. Disabling it resumes normal
focus handling; playback may pause if focus cannot be acquired. Headphone
disconnection still pauses playback. Other apps and the system may apply their own
audio policies, so this option cannot guarantee that every app will mix audio.

## Music library

The Music tab can use local storage, Jellyfin, or Navidrome as its source. Local browsing includes:

- Songs, albums, artists, and playlists
- Artwork-aware grid and list layouts
- Search and sorting
- Folder exclusions and a minimum-track-duration filter
- Favorites and shuffled playback

The dedicated now-playing view combines artwork, metadata, transport controls, and the up-next queue.

### Immersive artwork

Enable **Audio settings → Appearance → Immersive artwork** for edge-to-edge
album artwork that fades into the portrait player's background. It is off by
default. Switch from the visualizer to the artwork to see it; landscape keeps
the existing side-by-side layout, and opening lyrics keeps their readable
background. Tracks without artwork keep the regular placeholder.

This presentation is inspired by [Mornye](https://github.com/mornyeapp/Mornye).
The existing **Animated album artwork** switch remains separate. Reduced motion
keeps immersive artwork still, and unavailable motion artwork retains the
ordinary cover.

Album artwork does not seek or toggle play/pause on single or double taps.
Horizontal swipes still switch tracks, and a long press opens the visualizer
selector. Use the transport buttons and seekbar for playback control.

### Switch the source

Choose the active source from Music settings. Local uses Android media and
filesystem indexing; Jellyfin and Navidrome use their saved server account.
Changing source replaces the browsed catalog but does not merge remote and local
albums into one library.

If a remote source is unavailable, switch back to Local rather than deleting
the server. See [Jellyfin and Navidrome](/docs/library-and-streaming/jellyfin-and-navidrome)
for connection behavior.

### Playlists, favorites, and queue

Create local audio playlists, add selected tracks, or save the current queue as
a named playlist. Favorites are represented by a stable audio playlist and stay
in sync with the active music notification.

The up-next queue supports normal or shuffled playback. **Play Next** inserts
after the active track; **Add to Queue** appends. See [Playlists, queues, and history](/docs/library-and-streaming/playlists-and-history).

## Lyrics

mpvRx can display embedded or local lyrics and fetch online lyrics from LRCLIB. When several sources are available, switch sources from the lyrics view. Availability and timing quality depend on the track metadata and provider result.

Timed lyrics follow playback; plain text remains readable without synchronization.
Changing tracks cancels in-flight loading and source conversion so a slow result
from the previous song cannot replace the current lyrics. Optional translation
and Indic-script romanization remain best-effort transformations; keep the
original source available for comparison.

### Edit and synchronize lyrics

Tap the **Edit lyrics** pencil in the lyrics view or provider picker. You can
edit, insert, and delete lines, or choose **Paste lyrics** for plain text or LRC.
Select a line and tap **Set time** as it is sung; selection advances to the next
line. Timestamp buttons seek to their line, the `-0.1 s` / `+0.1 s` controls
fine-tune timing, and each line's menu supports manual timestamp editing.

For synced lyrics, time every line and keep timestamps in line order before
saving. **Clear all timestamps** converts the draft to plain lyrics. Unchanged
word timing, backing vocals, and source translations are preserved; changing
a line's text clears its obsolete word timing and translations.

Edits are saved privately inside MpvRX, not in audio tags or a neighboring
file. They survive restarts and take priority over embedded/local lyrics and
automatic online lookup for that track URI. Selecting an online source remains
possible. **Restore original lyrics** removes only that track's saved edits.
Moving a local file or changing its URI creates a different editing target.

Closing a changed draft asks before discarding it. If playback changes tracks
while editing, the draft continues to belong to the original track; live timing
and seeking are disabled so edits cannot accidentally be applied to the new song.
Audiobook text is not editable through this music-lyrics editor.

## Visualizers

Blob, Galaxy, Cuboid, and Particle modes are available, along with an optional audio-reactive seekbar. They are presentation choices, not audio processors, and do not change the decoded signal.

Galaxy and Particle emphasize many moving elements; Cuboid emphasizes geometry;
Blob uses a continuous organic surface. All use additional rendering resources,
so disable them when battery life, PiP, or thermal headroom is the priority.

## Background playback

Unified background playback keeps audio or video active while you return to browser screens. Android may require notification permission for media controls and may restrict background behavior under aggressive battery optimization.

The music player's background-playback button shows a localized on/off toast,
including after minimizing to the mini-player and expanding it again.
